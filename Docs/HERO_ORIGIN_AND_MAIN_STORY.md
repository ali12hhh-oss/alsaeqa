## 16. Companion Canon — Permanent Companion

The hero meets the permanent companion in **Stage 5**, immediately after freeing her from the enemies holding her captive. This is a fixed story event and takes precedence over earlier roadmap wording that places the first meaningful companion meeting later.

She is not a temporary follower. She has her own personal story, combat abilities, meaningful combat participation, dialogue, animations and cinematic development. Her family story remains a major arc, with the established family-search progression continuing through stages 25–40.

## 17. Temporary Companion Canon

Temporary companions are distinct characters who join the hero for specific high-intensity story stages where their skills, presence or circumstances justify needing an additional companion. They are not interchangeable copies of the permanent companion.

Their encounters should be placed according to story and stage needs, especially difficult combat, dangerous rescues, infiltration/escape, traversal challenges and major enemy strongholds. They may enter for a specific sequence, participate in combat and cinematics, and leave naturally when that story segment ends.

Where required by the story, their state must persist: rescued, alive, separated, lost, or available for later world interactions.

## 18. Companion Progression Rule

The campaign distinguishes:
1. **Hero** — central protagonist and Thunder heir.
2. **Permanent companion** — rescued in Stage 5, with her own story and combat role.
3. **Temporary companions** — encountered later at carefully selected high-intensity stages where additional skills or presence are narratively justified.

The number and timing of temporary companions must serve pacing and escalation rather than a repetitive schedule.
